## 1. Non-Custodial Nature of the Application

Kora Wallet is a fully non-custodial cryptocurrency wallet. This means:

- The developer does **NOT** have access to your private keys, seed phrases (recovery phrases), wallet addresses, PIN codes, or any other sensitive credentials at any time.
- The developer **CANNOT** initiate, authorize, modify, or reverse any transaction on your behalf.
- The developer **CANNOT** recover your wallet if you lose your seed phrase or PIN. Recovery is solely your responsibility.
- You retain full and exclusive control over your digital assets at all times.

## 2. Data Collection and Privacy

Kora Wallet does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data on external servers. Specifically:

- All private keys, seed phrases, and wallet credentials are stored exclusively on your local device using AES-256 encryption.
- No analytics, telemetry, usage statistics, or crash reports are transmitted to the developer or any third party.
- No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or processed.
- The application does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or similar technologies.

---

## 3. Blockchain Data and Third-Party Services

To provide wallet functionality, Kora Wallet connects to third-party public blockchain nodes and market data APIs. This involves:

- Transmitting your public wallet addresses to blockchain nodes to retrieve balance and transaction history. Public addresses are not personally identifiable in isolation.
- Retrieving cryptocurrency price data from third-party providers.
- No private keys or sensitive credentials are ever transmitted externally.

The developer is not responsible for the availability, accuracy, security, or privacy practices of any third-party service or API provider.

By using Kora Wallet, you accept full and sole responsibility for:

**a)** Safeguarding your seed phrase and PIN code. Loss of these credentials results in permanent and irrecoverable loss of access to your assets. The developer provides no backup, recovery, or support mechanism.

**c)** Ensuring compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, and tax obligations in your jurisdiction regarding cryptocurrency use.

**d)** Maintaining the physical and digital security of the device on which Kora Wallet is installed.
