Calvi AI: Calorie Tracker·App Store·Google Play Flutter, Node.js, PostgreSQL, LLM · 2026

Calorie tracker with an AI assistant: a meal is logged from a sentence, a voice note or a photo, and the week gets a review. Flutter app on its own Fastify and PostgreSQL back end; the diary works offline, and model keys never reach the phone. When a store webhook goes missing, the server asks RevenueCat itself, so a paid renewal is not cut off. Text and photo requests run through a chain of LLMs (DeepSeek, with automatic failover to a backup provider), and an evaluation bench runs conversation and photo cases against the live models and measures latency. Native Apple Watch app for voice logging, 13 languages, sign-in with Apple and Google, 375 test files across server and app. 475+ registered users.
