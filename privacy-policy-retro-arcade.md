# Privacy Policy — Retro Arcade

**Last updated:** December 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how **Uzor** (“we”, “our”, “us”) handles information in the mobile game **Retro Arcade**.

We respect your privacy. The app is designed to work **offline**, **without accounts**, **without ads**, and **without tracking**. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## 1. Data Collection

We **do not** collect, transmit, or access any personal information.
* We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
* We do not access your contacts, files, photos, or location.
* We do not use any third-party analytics SDKs.
* We do not use advertising SDKs.

## 2. Data Storage

All non-personal data, including **saved games, high scores, and application settings**, are stored **exclusively locally on your device**.
* We do not operate any external servers for gameplay storage.
* This local data is never backed up or transmitted by us.
* If you uninstall the app or clear app data, your progress will be permanently deleted from the device.

## 3. Children’s Privacy

Our game is safe for all ages. Since we do not collect any data whatsoever, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or trade any user data with third parties, as we do not collect any data in the first place.

## 5. Permissions

The app does not request sensitive permissions (such as Camera, Microphone, Location, or Contacts) to function.

## 6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if our practices change or legal requirements evolve. **The latest version of this policy will always be available for review at its permanent public link.**
